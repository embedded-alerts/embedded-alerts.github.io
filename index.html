Why it exists

An alert is a decision, not an email.

Keyword alerts force the recipient to perform relevance work after delivery. Embedded Alerts moves that work into a transparent matching pipeline: scoped ingestion, inspectable evidence, explicit thresholds, and stateful deduplication.
